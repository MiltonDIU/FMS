Research Division & Scopus Operations Guide

Faculty Management System (FMS) · Institutional Research & Incentives

In short: download university Scopus publication export → upload workbook under Scopus Operations → review author matches in the online disambiguation interface → bind Scopus Author IDs to faculty records → calculate and export financial incentive sheets for Accounts & Finance.

1. Uploading the Scopus Dataset

  1. Export institutional publications from Elsevier Scopus in CSV or Excel format. Ensure columns for Author Names, Affiliation, Title, DOI, Source Title, and Scopus Author ID are included.
  2. In FMS, navigate to Scopus Operations → Review Runs and click New Review Run.
  3. Upload the workbook. The engine automatically handles UTF-8 BOM, validates headers, and separates internal university authors from external international collaborators.

2. Online Disambiguation Review Interface

Because multiple faculty members may share common surnames or initials:

  • Open the active review run and click Online Review.
  • Automatic Matches: papers whose DOI or author Scopus ID already exists in FMS are matched with 100% confidence.
  • Candidate Selection: for ambiguous names, the interface suggests potential departmental candidates. Reviewers select the exact faculty member based on research discipline.

3. Binding Scopus Author IDs

When you confirm an author match in the review run:

  1. The teacher's official Scopus Author ID is permanently bound to their profile record.
  2. All future dataset uploads automatically recognize that author ID, eliminating manual disambiguation on future runs.
  3. The paper is added to the teacher's publications list and appears on their public directory CV.

4. Generating Research Incentive Workbooks

To reward faculty research under university incentive policies:

  • Navigate to Research Incentives.
  • Filter papers by publication year, index, quartile (Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4), or department.
  • The incentive engine calculates monetary rewards based on first author, corresponding author, and co-author split weightings.
  • Click Export Incentives Sheet to download an audited Excel workbook ready for submission to the university Finance Office.
