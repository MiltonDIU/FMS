Research Team Guide

Faculty Management System · Daffodil International University

In short: you own the university's publication record: enter and classify papers, record incentives, bring in Scopus exports under review, export data for reports, and keep the research directory.

1. Signing in

Sign in at /admin with your login email and password. Use Forgot password? on the sign-in page if needed. Your Dashboard shows publication charts by year, type, quartile, grant type, linkage and collaboration; click a figure to open the papers behind it.

3. Entering and correcting publications

  1. Open Publications and click New publication.
  2. Choose the faculty and department, then fill in the title, journal or conference, publication date or year, DOI and the other details.
  3. Classify it: type, quartile, grant type, linkage and research collaboration. Every paper needs all five so reports add up; use "N/Q" or "Not Assigned" where the value is not known.
  4. Under Authorship, choose the First Author, Corresponding Author and Co-Authors.
  5. Save. The paper appears on its DIU authors' public profiles.

To correct a paper, click Edit on its row. Use the filters (date range, type, quartile, grant type, linkage, collaboration, incentive status, source, faculty, department, internal teacher attached) to find papers that need work.

Teachers also add papers from their own profiles. When they name DIU colleagues, those colleagues must confirm. Papers you enter attach to their authors directly.

4. Authors

  • An author who is not a DIU teacher can be created on the spot: click New Author on the publication form and enter the name, email, category and Scopus author ID.
  • Authors lists every author. Filter by Ours, and not yet merged to find authors who wrote under DIU's affiliation but are not yet tied to a teacher.
  • If an author record turns out to be a DIU teacher, select it and use the bulk action Merge into Teacher; their papers move to the teacher.
  • The same person entered twice as an author: select both and use Merge Selected Authors, choosing which record survives.

5. Recording incentives

  1. On a paper's row in Publications, click Incentive, or open Incentives and click New.
  2. Enter the total amount in taka.
  3. Add each author's share, with their role: 1st Author, Corresponding Author or Co-Author. The shares are checked against the total.
  4. Set the status: Pending, Approved or Paid, and add remarks if useful.
  5. Save. Who created, approved and paid it is recorded, and every later change is kept in Incentive Logs.

Incentives can be filtered by faculty and department, status and publication date. To see what each teacher has received, use Export by Author.

6. Exporting for reports

  • On Publications: Export Publications (one row per paper) or Export by Author (one row per author and paper).
  • On Incentives: Export Incentives or Export by Author, the sheet for Finance.

Set the filters first; the export uses them. The file is built in the background and you are notified when it is ready to download.

7. Scopus Review

Brings papers from a Scopus export into FMS, reviewed paper by paper and author by author, instead of retyping them.

  1. Open Scopus Review and click Upload a Scopus export. Set the matching options and upload the file.
  2. Wait for the run to show Ready. Its row shows how many papers FMS already holds and how many are new, and how many DIU authors are teachers, external authors or unknown. Summary shows the same figures.
  3. Decide each paper and author, in one of two ways:
    • Review Online: decide in the browser, then click Import Decisions Online.
    • Download the workbook, fill in the Decision column in Excel, then use Import checked workbook to upload it.
  4. Approved papers are created and teachers' Scopus author IDs are linked, so future runs recognise them automatically.

If matching looks wrong, adjust Institution Identity and click Run again on the run. Only a Super Admin can delete a run.

8. Institution Identity

Sets the words a Scopus run is judged by: which affiliation text counts as DIU, which similarly named institutions are not DIU, and how a student's address is told apart from a staff member's. If nothing is ever changed here, the built-in DIU defaults apply. Wrong settings do not cause an error; the run simply recognises nobody, so check the counts after a change.

9. The research directory

  1. Open Teachers. Filter by In research directory to see who is in it.
  2. Turn the Researcher switch on or off on a teacher's row. The research website reads this list.
  3. Click Dashboard on a row to look at a teacher's profile figures before deciding.

You cannot edit teachers' profiles; this switch is the one thing you change about a teacher.

10. What you cannot do

  • Edit teacher profiles or approve profile changes.
  • Create accounts or change roles.
  • Send emails to teachers.
