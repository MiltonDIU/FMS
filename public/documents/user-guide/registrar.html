Registrar Office Guide

Faculty Management System · Daffodil International University

In short: you keep the official teacher record for the whole university: create and edit teacher records, record departures, fill fields from the ERP, and review the profile changes routed to you.

1. Signing in

Sign in at /admin with your login email and password. Use Forgot password? on the sign-in page if needed.

3. Adding a teacher

  1. Open Teachers and click New teacher.
  2. If the teacher was on the old website, search for them to bring their details across.
  3. Enter at least the employee ID, name, login email, department and designation, then save.
  4. Ask a Super Admin to send the activation email so the teacher can sign in.

4. Editing a teacher

  1. On Teachers, find the teacher (search by name, ID or email, or use the filters) and click Edit.
  2. You see the same tabs as the teacher, plus the fields only your office may change: department, designation, employee ID, login email, profile URL, joining date, work location, and the Settings tab (employment status, job type, profile status, publicly visible, active, archived).
  3. Save. Your edits to another teacher's profile apply immediately and are recorded in that profile's history. They do not wait for approval.

To take a profile off the public website without deleting it, turn off Publicly Visible on the Settings tab.

5. Recording a departure or return

  • Set Employment Status on the Settings tab. The Leaving Date opens for any status other than Active, and is required for Retired, Resigned and Terminated.
  • A retired, resigned or terminated teacher leaves the public directory but stays in the record.
  • When someone returns, set the status back to Active. The leaving date clears, a new period of service opens, and earlier periods are kept. Do not create a second profile.
  • The Teachers list can be filtered by leaving date, joining date and Rejoined.

6. Filling fields from the ERP

  1. On a teacher's row, click Fill from ERP.
  2. Choose the fields to fill and what to do when FMS already holds a value.
  3. Run it. The result says what was filled and why anything was not. These fills do not create approval requests.

On the edit page, Compare with ERP shows the FMS and ERP data side by side and lets you merge or override whole sections.

7. Reviewing profile changes

The university decides which tabs need approval and who approves each one. You decide the tabs routed to the Registrar Office.

  1. Open Approvals → Pending Approvals. The badge shows how many are waiting. Filter by teacher if needed.
  2. Click View. Changed items are opened, unchanged ones folded; each shows before and after.
  3. Decide:
    • Approve Changes or Approve All: the change goes live on the profile and website.
    • Approve Sections / Reject Sections: decide part of a submission.
    • Reject Changes or Reject All: a written reason is required and is sent to the teacher.

Decision buttons appear only for the tabs routed to you. Every decision is kept with who made it and when.

8. Rolling a profile back

  1. In Pending Approvals, filter to the teacher and find an approved version or restore point.
  2. Click Rollback and confirm.

The profile returns to that state, except Publications, which are left as they are. The rollback is recorded like any other change.

9. Sent and blocked emails

  • Sent Emails shows each message sent to teachers, and for every recipient whether it was delivered, opened and clicked.
  • An address that bounces should be added to Blocked Emails. The system sends nothing to it until it is corrected and unblocked.

10. Keeping the lists up to date

Faculties, departments, designations, job types, employment statuses and the degree lists feed the teacher forms and the public site. Add or rename items there rather than typing free text into profiles.

11. What you cannot do

  • Create user accounts or change roles (Admin).
  • Change approval rules or routing (Admin).
  • Send activation or bulk email (Super Admin).
  • Edit publications or incentives (Research Team).
