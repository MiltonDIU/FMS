Dean & Associate Dean Guide

Faculty Management System · Daffodil International University

In short: you see the teachers, publications and incentives of every department in your faculty, view only. You approve profile changes only if the university names you as an approver.

You are a teacher too. For your own profile, follow the Teacher Guide.

1. Signing in

Sign in at /admin with your login email and password. First time? Use the activation email, as described in the Teacher Guide. Use Forgot password? on the sign-in page if needed.

2. What you can see

Everything you see is limited to your faculty, across all its departments. This comes from your administrative post (for example "Dean, Faculty of Science and Information Technology"), which the administration records. If you see other faculties, or nothing, your post has not been recorded or has ended; ask an admin.

3. Following your faculty's teachers

  1. Open Teachers. Use the Department filter to look at one department at a time.
  2. Filter by Verification Status, employment status or Has Publications, and use the Completion Score column to see which profiles are incomplete.
  3. Click View on a row to read the full profile.

You cannot edit a teacher's profile. Teachers change their own; the Registrar Office changes the fields it owns. The public listing order of each department is set by its head.

4. Publications and incentives

  • Publications lists papers by your faculty's teachers. Filter by department, date range, type, quartile, grant type, linkage or collaboration.
  • Incentives shows the incentive amount for each paper, each author's share, and whether it is pending, approved or paid.
  • Incentive Logs shows every change to an incentive record, who made it and when.

These are view only. Corrections go to the Research Team.

5. Approving profile changes

Deans approve a tab of a teacher's profile only when the university has named the Dean role, or you personally, as an approver for it. Associate Deans do not approve. If you are an approver:

  1. Open the notification, or Pending Approvals.
  2. Open the submission and compare the before and after values.
  3. Click Approve Changes, or Reject Changes with a written reason the teacher will receive. You can also decide section by section.

You never approve changes to your own profile.

6. What you cannot do

  • See other faculties.
  • Edit teachers' profiles, publications or incentives.
  • Change a department's listing order.
  • Send emails or create accounts.
