FMS User Guides

Faculty Management System · Daffodil International University

Each guide covers one role: what you can do in FMS, step by step, and what is out of your hands. Open the guide for your role. If you hold more than one role, for example Head and Teacher, read both.

TeacherActivate your account, keep your profile up to date, add publications, confirm co-authorship and track approvals. Head & Associate HeadSee your department's teachers, publications and incentives, set the public listing order, and approve changes when routed. Dean & Associate DeanSee your faculty's teachers, publications and incentives across all its departments. Registrar OfficeCreate and maintain teacher records, review profile changes, and roll a profile back. Research TeamPublications, incentives, Scopus review, exports and the research directory. AdminUsers, roles, administrative posts, approval rules, routing, email templates and ERP mapping. Super AdminSystem settings, activation and bulk email, bulk import, completion scores and the activity log.

The roles in one table

RoleSeesIn one line
TeacherOwn profileKeeps their own profile and confirms papers they were added to
Associate HeadOwn departmentViews the department and sets its listing order
HeadOwn departmentSame as Associate Head, plus approves the department's profile changes when routed
Associate Dean / DeanOwn facultyViews the faculty's teachers, publications and incentives
RegistrarWhole universityMaintains teacher records and reviews profile changes
Research TeamWhole universityOwns publications, incentives, Scopus intake and the research directory
AdminWhole universityManages users, roles, approval rules and all records
Super AdminEverythingAll of the above, plus system settings, bulk email and imports
These guides describe the default role setup as of 18 October 2026. Your administrator can change what a role may do, so your screen may differ slightly.
