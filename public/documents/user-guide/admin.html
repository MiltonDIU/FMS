Faculty Management System · Daffodil International University

2. User accounts

  1. Open Users and click New user.
  2. Enter the name, email and a password, choose one or more roles, and leave Active on.
  3. Save.
  • A person can hold several roles. A head of department usually needs both Teacher and Head.
  • Teacher accounts are normally created with the teacher record and activated by email; you rarely create them here.
  • To stop someone signing in, turn Active off rather than deleting the account.

3. Roles and permissions

Roles lists the nine roles: Teacher, Associate Head, Head, Associate Dean, Dean, Registrar, Research Team, Admin and Super Admin. Open a role to tick or untick what it may do, page by page and action by action, and save.

Change roles carefully. Removing a permission can hide a page someone relies on; adding one can show data to people outside its unit.

4. Administrative posts (deans, heads)

A dean or head sees only their own faculty or department because of their post, recorded in Administrative Role Users, not because of the role alone.

  1. Open Administrative Role Users and click New.
  2. Choose the person and the administrative role (for example Dean, Head of Department, Associate Head, Program Coordinator).
  3. Choose the faculty (for faculty posts) or department (for department posts).
  4. Set the start date, mark Acting if it is an acting post, keep Active on, and save.
  5. Make sure the user also has the matching role (Dean, Head and so on) in Users.

When a term ends, set the end date. The person then loses the unit view and, for heads, approval of that department. The post also appears as a dated block on their public profile.

5. Approval Settings

One row per profile tab (Basic Information, Contact Information, Personal Details, Research Interest, Area of Expertise, Education, Publications, Job Experience, Training, Awards, Skills, Teaching Areas, Memberships, Social Links, Documents, Profile Settings). For each, choose:

  • Requires approval on: a teacher's change waits for a reviewer.
  • Requires approval off: it goes live when the teacher saves, and is still recorded in history.

6. Notification Routings

Decides who approves each tab. Only the people a tab is routed to can decide it; a senior role on its own is not enough.

  1. Open Notification Routings and click New.
  2. Choose the sections (leave empty for all sections).
  3. Choose the recipient type:
    • Role: everyone holding the chosen roles.
    • User: named people.
    • Department Head: the head of the teacher's own department only, never the teacher themselves.
  4. Keep it active and save.

Routed people are notified of each change and see decision buttons for those tabs in Pending Approvals. They also need permission to open Pending Approvals (view Teacher Versions) on their role.

7. Teacher records at scale

  • Fill Fields from ERP (Teachers page header): fills chosen fields for all teachers of the selected employment statuses. It shows how many teachers the run will call first, and reports the result.
  • Merge Selected (bulk action): when two profiles are the same person, select both, choose the profile to keep, and review what will move before confirming.
  • Researcher switch and Rollback work as in the Research Team and Registrar guides.

8. Email Templates

The wording of system emails, such as the activation email and approval notices. Edit the subject and body. Keep any placeholders already in a template, since the system fills them in for each recipient.

9. Integration Mappings

How fields in the HR/ERP data map onto FMS fields. Change a mapping when the ERP renames or moves a field, then test with one teacher using Compare with ERP before a bulk fill.

10. Lists and lookups

Faculties, departments, designations, administrative roles and the other lists feed every form and the public site. Rename an item rather than creating a near-duplicate. Research Projects is ready for use when the university starts entering projects.

11. What only a Super Admin can do

  • System Settings (maintenance mode, ERP credentials, mail, theme, branding).
  • Send activation emails and bulk email to teachers.
  • Import teachers in bulk, Team Directory, Activity Log.
  • Calculate completion scores in bulk, switch Login Allowed, restore photos.
