Head & Associate Head Guide

Faculty Management System · Daffodil International University

In short: you see your own department's teachers, publications and incentives; you set the order your teachers appear on the department's public page; and, as Head, you approve your teachers' profile changes when the university routes them to you.

You are a teacher too. For your own profile, follow the Teacher Guide. Changes to your own profile always go to other reviewers; you never approve yourself.

1. Signing in

Sign in at /admin with your login email and password. First time? Use the activation email, as described in the Teacher Guide. Use Forgot password? on the sign-in page if needed.

2. What you can see

Everything you see is limited to your department. This comes from your administrative post (for example "Head of Department, CSE"), which the administration records. If you see other departments, or nothing, your post has not been recorded or has ended; ask an admin.

3. Following your department's teachers

  1. Open Teachers. Only your department's teachers are listed.
  2. Use Filters to find who needs a reminder, for example by Verification Status (not yet confirmed), employment status, or Has Publications. The Completion Score column shows how complete each profile is.
  3. Click View on a row to read the full profile.

You cannot edit a teacher's profile. Teachers change their own; the Registrar Office changes the fields it owns.

4. Setting the public listing order

You decide the order your teachers appear in on your department's page of the faculty website.

  1. Open Department Teachers.
  2. Filter the list to your department. Reordering is available only while one department is selected.
  3. Click the reorder button above the table, drag rows into the order you want, and finish reordering.

The website follows the new order straight away.

5. Publications and incentives

  • Publications lists papers by your department's teachers. Filter by date range, type, quartile, grant type, linkage or collaboration.
  • Incentives shows the incentive amount for each paper, each author's share, and whether it is pending, approved or paid.
  • Incentive Logs shows every change to an incentive record, who made it and when.

These are view only. Corrections go to the Research Team.

6. Approving profile changes (Head only)

The university decides who approves each tab of a teacher's profile. When a tab is routed to "Department Head", you approve that tab for your own department's teachers. Associate Heads do not approve.

  1. You get a notification when a teacher in your department submits a change you must decide. Click it, or open Pending Approvals.
  2. Open the submission. Changed items are opened for you; each shows the value before and after.
  3. Decide:
    • Approve Changes: the change goes live on the profile and the website.
    • Reject Changes: write the reason. The teacher receives it and can correct and resubmit.
    • To decide part of a submission, approve or reject section by section.

You see the decision buttons only for the tabs routed to you. If you are told you are an approver but cannot open Pending Approvals, ask the administration to check your access.

7. What you cannot do

  • See other departments.
  • Edit teachers' profiles, publications or incentives.
  • Approve your own profile.
  • Send emails or create accounts.
