Department Head & Dean Guide

Faculty Management System (FMS) · Academic Review Workflow

In short: a faculty member edits their profile → changes are held in a pending version → you receive an in-app notification → open Teacher Versions → compare proposed changes with previous records → click Approve Version (or Reject Version with remarks) → the live website and teacher's CV update automatically.

1. The Review Workflow

To preserve institutional data authenticity, faculty profile edits to sensitive sections (qualifications, job titles, experiences) do not immediately overwrite the live public directory. Instead, they are held as a pending version waiting for your approval.

Public Directory Protection: While a version is pending your review, public visitors to the university website continue seeing the previously approved profile. No unverified claims are published without head/dean oversight.

2. How to review a teacher's submission

  1. Sign in to the admin panel at http://localhost:8000/admin.
  2. In the left sidebar, click Teacher Versions.
  3. You will see a list of pending submissions from faculty members in your department.
  4. Click on a version to view the review screen.
  5. The screen shows side-by-side differences:
    • + Green: newly added degrees, experiences, or publications.
    • - Red: removed items.
    • • Yellow: modified values (e.g. passing year, grade, or institution name).

3. Approving or Rejecting a submission

ActionWhen to useWhat happens
Approve Version Information is verified, accurate, and matches university records. The new data is merged into the teacher's active profile, published to the web directory, and a rollback restore point is recorded.
Reject Version Information is inaccurate, incomplete, or requires revision. You enter mandatory remarks (e.g. "Please attach correct passing year"). The teacher is notified and instructed to correct their profile.

4. The Self-Review Guard

Conflict of Interest Rule: As Department Head, you cannot review or approve your own personal profile revisions. When you update your own profile under My Profile, the review request is automatically routed to your Faculty Dean or Central Administration.

5. Monitoring Department Completeness & Roster

From the Teachers directory table, filter by your department code to monitor:

  • Profile Completion Scores: see which teachers have high scores vs who has incomplete mandatory information.
  • Active vs Departed Staff: confirm that only current faculty are marked active in the directory.
  • Publications Count: keep track of research output across your academic department.
