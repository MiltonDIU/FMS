1. Signing in

The first time

  1. Open the activation email from FMS and click its link. The link works once, and only for the number of days stated in the email.
  2. You are signed in and taken to Set password. Choose a password; each rule is ticked as you meet it.
  3. You are taken into the panel. From now on you sign in with your login email and this password.

Every time after that

  • Go to /admin on the FMS site and sign in.
  • Forgot your password? Click Forgot password? on the sign-in page and follow the email.
  • Link expired or already used? Ask the Registrar Office for a new activation email.

2. What is in your menu

PageWhat it is for
DashboardQuick actions, your profile figures, and any co-authorship requests waiting for you
My ProfileYour whole profile, where you make changes
My Profile HistoryEvery change to your profile and what happened to it
PublicationsEvery paper you are attached to (view only)
Assigned to MePapers colleagues added you to, waiting for your answer or already answered

The bell icon at the top shows notifications: approvals, rejections with the reviewer's reason, and co-authorship requests. Each one links to what it is about. View Site in the user menu (top right) opens the public website.

3. Updating your profile

  1. Open My Profile. The tabs down the left side are:
    TabWhat goes in it
    Basic InfoPhoto, name, biography. Department, designation, employee ID and similar are shown but locked.
    Contact InfoPhones, PBX extension, office room, secondary email, addresses
    Personal DetailsDate of birth, gender, blood group, nationality, religion
    Research InterestYour research interests
    Area of ExpertiseYour areas of expertise
    EducationsEach degree: level, type, institution, country, year, and result (CGPA and scale, or marks and total marks)
    PublicationsYour papers and their authors (see section 7)
    Job ExperiencePrevious posts and organisations, with dates
    Training ExperienceTrainings, workshops and courses
    AwardsAwards, honours and scholarships
    SkillsTechnical and professional skills
    Teaching AreasSubjects you teach
    MembershipsProfessional bodies
    Social LinksGoogle Scholar, ORCID, ResearchGate, LinkedIn, your website and so on
    DocumentsCertificates and other documents
    SettingsEmployment status, job type and visibility, shown for information only
  2. Change what you need. Inside a tab, use Add to add another degree, job, award and so on, and the delete icon on a row to remove it.
  3. At the bottom of the page, tick the accuracy declaration. Save Changes stays disabled until you do. The tick is needed on every save.
  4. Click Save Changes. You will see one of:
    • Profile update submitted successfully: your changes are saved (see the next section for when they go live).
    • No changes to save: nothing was different from what is already stored.
    • An error that names the tab and the field to correct.

Fields you cannot change yourself: department, designation, employee ID, login email, profile URL, joining date, work location, and everything on the Settings tab. The Registrar Office owns these; ask them for a correction.

There are no drafts. Every save is sent at once. To change something you already submitted, edit it and save again.

4. What happens after you save

The university decides which tabs need a reviewer's approval. A tab that does not need approval goes live as soon as you save. A tab that does waits for its reviewer, and until then the public site keeps showing your previous details.

5. Confirming your profile once

Most profiles were brought over from the old website. Until you confirm yours, My Profile shows a confirmation panel.

  1. Go through every tab and correct anything wrong. Save your corrections.
  2. In the confirmation panel, tick the box and click the confirm button.

This is asked only once. It marks your profile as verified, which the university may require before a profile appears in the public directory.

6. Your completion score

  • The banner at the top of My Profile shows how complete your profile is, from 0 to 100%.
  • Open the metrics to see what is missing. Each Needs Attention item takes you straight to the field.
  • The score counts what is saved and approved. After a reviewer approves your changes, click Refresh score to recalculate it.

7. Publications and co-authors

Adding a paper

  1. Go to My Profile → Publications and click Add.
  2. Enter the title, journal or conference, year, DOI and type.
  3. Under Authorship, choose the First Author, Corresponding Author and Co-Authors.
  4. Tick the declaration and save.

If you name a DIU colleague as an author, they are asked to confirm. The paper appears on their profile only after they do, and you are notified of their answer.

When a colleague adds you

  1. The request appears on your Dashboard, with a notification and a count beside Assigned to Me.
  2. Click Confirm if you are an author: the paper joins your profile and your counts.
  3. Click Decline if you are not: you will not be listed on it.

Assigned to Me keeps the full record: who added you, as what, when, and your answer.

Papers entered by the Research Team or brought in from Scopus attach to you directly, without a request. If a paper is wrong or missing, contact the Research Team.

8. Compare with ERP

If the university has switched this on, a Compare with ERP button appears at the top of My Profile. It needs your employee ID to be on your profile.

  1. Click it. Your FMS data is shown beside your ERP record, section by section, with differences marked.
  2. Choose Merge (fills only empty fields and adds records you do not have yet) or Override (replaces the chosen sections with the ERP data).
  3. Tick the sections to bring in and click Import & Synchronize.

9. Your profile history

My Profile History lists every change to your profile: waiting, approved, rejected, or saved at once. Open a row to see what changed, before and after, and any reviewer remarks. Only you and the reviewing offices can see your history.

10. Your public profile

  • Your profile appears on the faculty website under your faculty and department, and in the site search.
  • Visitors can download your CV as a PDF and your contact card (vCard), when the university has these switched on.
  • Each of your publications has its own page with ready citations (APA, IEEE, BibTeX).
  • Use View Site in the user menu to check how your profile looks.
  • Not showing? A profile is public only when it is active, not archived and marked publicly visible. Ask the Registrar Office.
