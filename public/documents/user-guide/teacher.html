Teacher / Faculty Member Guide

Faculty Management System (FMS) · Daffodil International University

In short: receive activation email → set your password → sign in to /admin → open My Profile → complete the 13 profile tabs → add degrees with division/marks → add publications and name co-authors → confirm papers in "Assigned to Me" → tick the Accuracy Declaration checkbox → click Save Changes → once audited by your Department Head, click REFRESH SCORE to update your percentage ring.

1. Activating your account

  1. The Registrar or ICT team creates your account and sends an activation email with a secure link.
  2. Open the email and click Activate Faculty Profile.
  3. Set a permanent password (at least 8 characters) and confirm it.
  4. Go to http://localhost:8000/admin and sign in using your official institutional email and password.

3. Entering Educational Qualifications (Marks & Division)

Under the Educations tab, click Add Education for each degree:

  • Choose the Degree Type (e.g. B.Sc., M.Sc., Ph.D., H.S.C, S.S.C).
  • Select or type the Educational Institution and Country.
  • Choose the Result Type:
    • CGPA / Grade: enter your CGPA (e.g. 3.85) and Scale (e.g. 4.00).
    • First / Second Division or Class: enter your marks obtained (e.g. 750) and Total Marks (e.g. 1000). Both fields are supported.
  • Select your passing year and duration.

4. Adding Publications & Co-Authors

  1. Open the Publications tab and click Add to Publications.
  2. Enter the title, journal/conference name, year, volume, issue, and DOI.
  3. Specify authorship roles:
    • First Author: select yourself or another colleague.
    • Corresponding Author: select who handles publication correspondence.
    • Co-authors: select all contributing DIU faculty members from the dropdown.
Co-author notification: When you select a colleague as a co-author, they receive a notification. The paper waits in their "Assigned to Me" tab for their confirmation before appearing on their public profile.

5. Confirming papers added by colleagues ("Assigned to Me")

If another teacher adds a publication and lists you as an author, you will see it in the Assigned to Me tab inside Publications:

  • Review the paper title and who added you.
  • Click Confirm if you are an author — the publication immediately appears on your CV.
  • Click Decline if you were tagged by mistake — you will not be listed.

6. Saving your profile (The Accuracy Declaration)

Whenever you make changes to your profile:

  1. Scroll down to the bottom of the page.
  2. You will notice the Save Changes button is in a Disabled state by default.
  3. Directly above the button, read the Profile Information Accuracy Declaration box.
  4. Tick the checkbox: "I hereby declare and confirm that all information, credentials, experiences, and records provided across all sections of my profile are authentic, accurate, and completely up to date."
  5. The Save Changes button will immediately become Active.
  6. Click Save Changes.
Why is the checkbox required every time? Under institutional governance rules, you take personal legal responsibility for the authenticity of your submitted degrees, marks, and publications. Each time you save, confirming the checkbox is mandatory.

7. Understanding your Profile Score & the REFRESH SCORE button

At the top of your profile is the Overall Integrity & Assessment Banner:

  • Score Percentage (0–100%): shows your profile completeness based on UGC and university accreditation requirements.
  • SHOW METRICS & GAPS: click this button to open the audit checklist. It shows which tabs have empty mandatory or optional fields, with a GO › button to jump right to each field.
  • HIDE METRICS & GAPS: click this button (beside the score ring) to collapse the panel.
  • REFRESH SCORE: whenever you update your profile or after your Department Head approves your updates, click REFRESH SCORE. The system recalculates your percentage ring in real time.
Why doesn't the score jump up immediately upon saving? FMS uses an approval review system. When you submit new degrees or designations, they undergo review by your Department Head first. Only approved, verified data contributes to your official score.

8. Syncing with ERP

If your official records (promotions, contact details, degrees) have been updated in the university ERP:

  1. Click the Sync with ERP button at the top header of My Profile.
  2. A comparison window will show your current FMS records on the left and ERP data on the right.
  3. You can inspect differences and apply updates to merge them directly into your profile.
